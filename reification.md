### The outputs of a statistical model are statements about the model, not about the real world.

This principle refers to the fallacy of *statistical reification*, as defined by Sander Greenland (2017).  A more general form of this fallacy is discussed by Erica Thompson (2022).

### References

S. Greenland, 2017:  The need for cognitive science in methodology.  *American Journal of Epidemiology*, 186 (6):  639-645.

E. Thompson, 2022:  *Escape from Model Land: How Mathematical Models Can Lead Us Astray and What We Can Do About It*.  Basic Books.
